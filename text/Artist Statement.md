# Artist Statement

I am a Chinese artist based in Denmark.

I cut apart my own calligraphy and reassemble it.

My art is deeply rooted in calligraphy. In my early practice I wrote large cursive characters with oversized brushes. The writing is instantaneous and irreversible, and most attempts fail. As those failures accumulated, I began to develop a working process of collage.

My daily practice is my material. I cut the characters into strokes and fragments, then assemble them following a logic inspired by cursive writing. The brush leaves the paper between strokes, yet the movement continues across the gap. I search for connections across unrelated fragments, different sessions, sometimes years apart. The accidental fit excites me: two pieces aligning so perfectly they seem to have always belonged together, or a third laid over the join, so the eye completes what was never there.

My work is conceptual and process-driven. I write, cut, and assemble, following a fixed procedure, yet the outcome keeps surprising me, taking me beyond my visual boundaries. Calligraphy collage is my attempt to explore what contemporary calligraphy could become. Calligraphy is flat, but once overlaid, it gains depth. The cut deconstructs tradition, and the reassembly brings it into the realm of contemporary art. The strokes remain cultural signs, while the work as a whole steps toward Western abstraction.
