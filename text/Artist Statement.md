# Artist Statement

I am an artist based in Denmark.

I cut apart my own calligraphy and reassemble it.

My art is deeply rooted in Chinese calligraphy. In my early practice I wrote large cursive characters with an oversized brush. The writing is instantaneous and irreversible, and most attempts fail. As those failures accumulated over the years, I began to develop a working process of collage.

My daily practice is my material. I cut the characters into individual strokes and fragments, then assemble them following a logic inspired by cursive writing. The brush leaves the paper between strokes, yet the movement continues across the gap. I search for continuity across fragments from unrelated pieces, different sessions, sometimes years apart. The accidental fit excites me: two pieces aligning so perfectly they seem to have always belonged together, or a third laid over the join, so the eye completes what was never there.

My work is conceptual and process-driven. I write, cut, and assemble, following a fixed procedure, yet the outcome keeps surprising me, taking me beyond my own visual boundaries. Calligraphy collage is my ongoing attempt to explore what contemporary calligraphy could become. Calligraphy is flat, but once cut and overlaid, it gains depth. The cut deconstructs the tradition, and the reassembly brings it into the realm of contemporary art. The strokes remain cultural signs, while the work as a whole steps toward Western abstraction.
